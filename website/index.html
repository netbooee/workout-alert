Work out with your people.

Sigamos is Spanish for “let’s keep going.” Keep a weekly workout streak alive with friends and family: workouts sync from Apple Health, your partners cheer you on, and Lotl the axolotl keeps you honest.

Join the iPhone beta How it works

Requires iPhone and the free TestFlight app. Works with Apple Watch and any app that saves workouts to Apple Health.

Built for showing up, together.

Pick how many days a week you want to move. Sigamos counts them, and the people who know you best keep you going.

🔥

Weekly streaks

Hit your goal of 1–7 days and the streak grows. Freezes cover a missed week, and Rest mode pauses everything when you’re sick or traveling.

🤝

Accountability partners

Invite friends and family with a code. You see each other’s workouts and progress, and nobody else does.

✅

Check-ins you can verify

Add a photo or note to any workout. Partners react, verify, comment, or send a quick voice message.

🎁

Incentives

“Hit 5 workout days this week and coffee’s on me.” Partners offer real-world rewards for extra effort, and Sigamos tracks it.

⚡

XP, levels & a weekly league

Earn XP for every workout, check-in, and goal. Climb the league with your partners and build friend streaks.

👋

Nudges that help

Nudge a partner who’s behind, get a reminder when your streak is on the line, and celebrate together when you make it.

A look inside.

Light and dark mode, synced straight from your Watch.

Meet Lotl.

A pink axolotl who greets you each morning with what matters today. Lotl’s mood follows your week: playful, never shaming.

🔒 Only your partners

Your workouts, photos, and messages are visible to the partners you accept, and no one else.

Sigamos has no ads or trackers, and never sells or shares your data for marketing.
