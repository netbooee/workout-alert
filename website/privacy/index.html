Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The short version

Sigamos is a workout accountability app. We collect what’s needed to run it: your account, the workouts and activity you choose to share from Apple Health, and what you post (check-ins, comments, voice messages, challenges).

Your workout data is visible only to you and the partners you accept. If you choose to join public leagues, other players in your weekly group see only your first name (or a nickname), your buddy, your weekly XP and your league, never your workouts. We don’t show ads, we don’t use trackers, and we never sell your data or use your Health data for advertising or marketing.

This policy explains how Antonio Martinez (“Sigamos,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use the Sigamos iPhone app and the website at sigamos.cloud. Questions? Email netbooee@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address, used to sign in with a one-time code and for account emails.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google: if you use one of these, we receive an identifier for your account and, if you allow it, your name and email (Apple lets you hide your email).
  • Profile: your display name, an optional profile photo, your buddy, weekly goal, which workouts count, time zone, invite code, notification settings, and whether Rest mode is on.

Apple Health data (only with your permission)

If you allow it, Sigamos reads the following from Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health.

  • Workouts: activity type, start and end time, duration, active energy, distance, average and maximum heart rate during the workout, and the name of the app or device that recorded it (for example “Jordan’s Apple Watch”).
  • Daily totals: steps, active energy, exercise minutes, and resting heart rate.

Your daily step count also earns bonus XP. Steps are never shown to other people, only the XP they add to your total.

You can change what Sigamos can read at any time in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → Sigamos) or in iPhone Settings. Workouts you delete in Apple Health are removed from Sigamos the next time it syncs.

Things you create or share

  • Workouts you log by hand, check-in photos, and notes.
  • Comments and voice messages (up to 30 seconds, recorded in the app with your microphone permission).
  • Reactions, verifications, nudges, partner requests, and challenges (including the reward and notes you write).
  • If you join public leagues: that you’ve joined, an optional nickname, your league, and your weekly placings. If you report another player, we keep the report.

Information from your device

  • A push notification token, if you turn on notifications, so we can send nudges and updates to your phone.
  • Some settings are stored only on your device (for example light/dark appearance and whether you’ve seen the daily greeting).
  • Share cards (images of a workout, streak or league result) are made on your phone. We don’t receive what you share or where you share it.

Sigamos does not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifier, and it does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use information

  • To run the app: sign you in, sync your workouts and steps, calculate your streak, XP, leagues, and challenges, and show your activity to your partners.
  • If you join public leagues, to place you in a weekly group with people in nearby time zones (we use your time zone, not your location) and move you between leagues.
  • To send notifications you’ve turned on (nudges, partner activity, verifications, comments, challenges, and streak reminders) and sign-in code emails.
  • To keep Sigamos secure and working, and to respond when you contact us.

Apple Health data is used only to provide Sigamos’s fitness features. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we do not sell it or share it with data brokers or advertisers.

Who can see your information

  • Your accepted partners can see your profile (including your profile photo, if you add one), streak, weekly progress, workouts (including Health details like duration and heart rate), check-in photos and notes, and your reactions.
  • Comments and voice messages are visible to their author and the author’s partners (which always includes the workout’s owner).
  • Challenges are visible only to the two partners in them.
  • Public leagues (only if you join): up to about 20 other players in your weekly group see your first name or nickname, your buddy, your XP that week, your place and your league. They don’t see your profile photo, workouts, photos, streak, steps, partners or anything else. You can leave at any time.
  • Things you share yourself: when you share a card to Instagram, Messages or elsewhere, the image and caption (which includes your invite link) go where you send them, under that service’s terms.
  • Someone with a pending partner request with you can see your display name and profile photo, but not your activity.
  • Nobody else can see your activity. These rules are enforced in our database, not just in the app.

Storage and security

Your data is stored in the United States. It is encrypted in transit, profile photos, check-in photos and voice messages are kept in private storage and shared through short-lived links, and database access rules limit each person to their own data and their partners’. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you about a breach as required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete individual items at any time: your profile photo, workouts you logged by hand, check-in photos, comments, and voice messages. Workouts from Apple Health are removed when you delete them in the Health app. Removing a partner stops sharing between you.

To delete your account and all associated data, go to Me → Settings → Delete account in the app. Your account, workouts, profile and check-in photos, voice messages, comments, partners, challenges, XP and league history are deleted immediately. If you signed in with Apple, you’ll confirm with Apple once more and we revoke Sigamos’s access to your Apple ID. Data in Apple Health is never touched. You can also email netbooee@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, and we’ll delete it within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn Apple Health access, notifications, the microphone, and the camera on or off at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Choose which notifications you get in Me → Settings.
  • Join or leave public leagues, and change your league nickname, in Me → Settings → Privacy.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have additional rights; we’ll honor requests as the law requires.

Children

Sigamos is for people 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above, and for significant changes we’ll let you know in the app or by email.
