Getting started

How do I get Sigamos?

Sigamos is in a free beta for iPhone. Install Apple’s TestFlight app, then open the beta link on sigamos.cloud on your iPhone and tap Install.

My sign-in code didn’t arrive.

Codes come from noreply@mail.sigamos.cloud and usually arrive within a minute. Check your spam or promotions folder, and make sure the email address is spelled right. If it still doesn’t come, wait a minute before asking for another, or use Sign in with Apple.

Which workouts count?

Any workout saved to Apple Health counts: Apple Watch, the Fitness app, Strava, Peloton, and more. You can also log a workout by hand with + Log on Home, for any time in the last 14 days. You choose the shortest workout that counts (and a separate bar for walks) in Me → You → What counts. A day counts toward your weekly goal when it has a workout that counts.

How do I earn XP?

Workouts that count earn 10–40 XP depending on length, adding a photo earns +25, hitting your weekly goal +50, a partner verifying your workout +5, and finishing a challenge +100. Daily steps add a bonus too: +5 at 5,000 steps, +10 more at 8,000 and +10 more at 12,000. Tap your XP on Home to see it all. Steps earn XP only; they don’t count toward your streak.

Apple Health

My workouts aren’t showing up.

Open the Health app → your profile picture → Apps → Sigamos, and turn on all the categories. Then open Sigamos and pull down on Home to sync. If something still looks missing, go to Me → Refresh from Apple Health.

Does Sigamos change my Health data?

No. Sigamos only reads the data you allow and never writes to Apple Health.

Your profile

How do I add a profile photo?

Go to Me and tap your picture at the top. Take a photo or choose one from your library, and crop it to a square. Your partners see it instead of your initials. Players in public leagues never see it; they see your buddy. Tap your picture again to change or remove it.

Partners

How do I add a partner?

Go to Partners → + Add and share your invite link or 6-character code, or type in theirs. Once they accept, you can see each other’s workouts and progress.

How do I cancel a request I sent?

On the Partners tab, under Waiting for them to accept, tap Cancel next to their name.

How do I remove a partner?

On the Partners tab, press and hold their card, then tap Remove. You’ll stop seeing each other’s activity.

How do challenges work?

Tap 🎯 Challenge on a partner. Either reward them for a stretch goal, like “4 workout days this week → coffee on me,” or do it together and count both of your workouts toward one goal. Sigamos tracks progress automatically, and finishing earns +100 XP. Rewards are between you; Sigamos doesn’t handle money.

Public leagues

What are public leagues?

An optional weekly competition beyond your partners. Join from Partners → 🏆 Leagues. Your first XP each week puts you in a group of up to 20 people in nearby time zones. At the end of the week the top players move up a league (Spark → Ember → Flame → Blaze → Inferno) and the bottom few move down.

What do other players see?

Only your first name (or a nickname you choose), your buddy, your XP that week and your league. Never your workouts, photos, streak, steps or partners. To keep it fair, hand-logged workouts without a photo count half in public leagues.

How do I leave, or report someone?

Open Leagues and tap Leave public leagues at the bottom (or go to Me → Settings → Privacy). To report spam or cheating, tap the player in the table and choose Report.

Sharing

How do I share a workout or my streak?

Tap Share on one of your workouts or on the streak screen, or when you hit a streak milestone or move up a league. Sigamos makes a picture you can post to Instagram Stories, send in Messages, or save. It only includes your own numbers, plus your invite link so friends can join you.

Notifications

I’m not getting notifications.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Sigamos and make sure notifications are allowed. In the app, Me → Settings lets you choose which kinds you get.

Your account

How do I pause my streak while I’m sick or traveling?

Turn on Rest mode in Me → You. Missed weeks won’t break your streak or use a freeze, and reminders pause.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Me → Settings → Delete account. Everything is deleted right away. If you signed in with Apple, you’ll confirm with Apple once more so we can revoke Sigamos’s access to your Apple ID. Your Apple Health data isn’t touched. You can also email netbooee@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. See the Privacy Policy for details.
